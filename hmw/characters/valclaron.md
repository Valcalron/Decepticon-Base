<h2>Bio</h2>

<p>
Once a young Decepticon aiming to make a name for himself, Valclaron began by altering his appearance, adding armor and weaponry to bolster his chances in the gladiatorial arena. His path led him to the darkest, most brutal circuits of Cybertron, where he faced off with fierce opponents for the prize of victory and a share of Energon. Battle after battle, Valclaron amassed both experience and resources, allowing him to purchase advanced upgrades to his body and weapons.
</p>

<p>
As he climbed the ranks, he caught the attention of well-known gladiators, such as Omine No Kyo, Omega Destroyer, and War Hammer, sparring and fighting alongside them. Yet, when the opportunity came to challenge a reigning champion, he leaped at the chance despite warnings from his comrades. He received the best armor and weapons available, his optics set firmly on victory.
</p>

<p>
Declared <em>deactivated</em> at the scene, Valclaron was later revived en route to the smelting pits. Still bleeding Energon, he fought his way to a medical facility, vowing to himself that fame alone was no longer his purpose. Word spread of his survival, and tales circulated that even "The Mighty Slagmaker" couldn't kill him.
</p>

<p>
This rumor didn't sit well with Megatron, who attempted to finish Valclaron off. An Autobot distraction allowed Valclaron to escape back underground.
</p>

<p>
With the Decepticons reclaiming Cybertron, Megatron brought the gladiator pits above ground, once again putting Valclaron in his line of sight. Rather than seeking his demise, Megatron ordered Soundwave to recruit Valclaron into the Decepticon army, where he willingly joined. Now, under Thundertron's command, he uses his gladiator skills to lethal effect against the Autobots.
</p>

<hr>

<h2>Currently</h2>

<p>
Valclaron remains under Thundertron’s watchful optic. His experience in close combat makes him deadly in hand-to-hand fighting, although he lacks long-range weapon training. His ego is bolstered by Thundertron’s status as Megatron’s brother, and he takes every opportunity to spar with his leader, often to the point of needing extensive repairs.
</p>

<p>
Though he refuses to speak, his silence has become a source of intrigue. Valclaron communicates through a form of sign language understood even by Transformers unfamiliar with him. His armor is scarred and cracked, revealing his battle-worn past—a look that belies his ageless, inscrutable nature. He is one of the most experienced warriors, weaving through Autobot ranks with his sword, leaving only smoke and spilled Energon behind.
</p>

<h2>Weapons</h2>

<p><strong>Robot Mode Primary:</strong> Energon War Gauntlet and Bone Sword.</p>
<p><strong>Secondary:</strong> Concealed liquid hydrogen incendiary emitters.</p>

<p><strong>Dragon Mode Primary:</strong> Brute Strength and (natural weapons).</p>
<p><strong>Dragon Mode Secondary:</strong> Hydrogen-powered fire breath.</p>

<hr>

<h2>Abilities</h2>

<p>
Valclaron's dragon form developed later, adding ferocity and primal instinct to his combat style. When in this form, his stats multiply by four, making him a terrifying opponent. His hydrogen fire breath can melt nearly any metal, with his robot mode utilizing this in the form of incendiary emitters.
</p>

<h2>Weaknesses</h2>

<p>
Valclaron’s battle-worn armor is loosely fitted, leaving vital areas exposed to greater impact. His dragon form’s strength and speed, while formidable, often risk damage to his internal systems. His hydrogen cells, kept on his back for both protection and emitter cooling, are his most vulnerable point. A direct hit could cause catastrophic damage, possibly leading to his complete destruction.
</p>
